How to use

1. Build your signature

  1. Fill in every field below, then press Generate.
  2. Use the checkboxes to drop anything you don't need — spacing closes up on its own, and whatever is switched off is removed from the copied signature entirely.
  3. Check the two previews at the top of the page. The second one sits inside a red 500×262 frame — that is the real size of the Pipedrive editor. Nothing may stick out of it.

2a. Gmail

  1. Press Copy for Gmail.
  2. In Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  3. Create a signature or pick an existing one, click inside the editing box and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V).
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the settings page and press Save Changes.

Paste straight into the settings box. Going through a compose window first can re-encode the images.

2b. Pipedrive

Read this before you paste. Pipedrive does not keep the name where it belongs. On paste it pulls the name image out of the layout and drops it at the very bottom of the block, below everything else. The signature will look broken and it will have no name on it. This is expected — you have to put the name back by hand: drag it up to the right of the photo, on the line above the job title, next to the blue badge. Nothing else needs moving.

  1. Press Copy for Pipedrive. This is a different, simplified version — the Gmail one will not survive Pipedrive's editor.
  2. In Pipedrive: Personal preferences → Email sync → Signature.
  3. Click inside the editor and paste.
  4. Put the name back. Scroll to the bottom of the pasted block — the name image is sitting there on its own. Drag it up to the right of the photo, onto the line directly above the job title, so it lands to the left of the blue badge.
  5. If the size looks off, click the image and drag its corner handle.
  6. Save, then reload the page to confirm the signature stayed intact, and send yourself a test email.

What Pipedrive changes, and why

Its editor is a rich-text field, not an HTML one. On paste it strips font sizes, font families and link underline settings, so:

  • Text uses the editor's own font at its own size. You can adjust it with the Pipedrive toolbar — its scale is 10 / 13 / 14 / 18 / 24 / 32.
  • Keep it at 18 or below. At 24 the contact icons no longer fit on one line and wrap.
  • Links come out underlined. Nothing can be done about that from our side.
  • The name is an image, so its font is unaffected.

If something looks wrong

  • Name missing — it is at the bottom of the pasted block. Drag it up next to the photo. Pipedrive always does this; there is no way to paste it in place.
  • Icons on a second line — the font size is above 18. Lower it.
  • Broken avatar — Avatar image name must match the file name on the CDN, without the .png extension.
  • Images vanished after saving — reload before you panic; if they are really gone, paste again.
